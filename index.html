What is Journey of Self-Discovery?

Sahaja Yoga is a simple, living method of meditation founded by Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi. Through the gentle awakening of our inner energy — the Kundalini — we learn to quiet the mind and become truly thoughtless.

The result is a deep, natural peace that spreads into every part of life: less stress, clearer attention, better sleep, warmer relationships and a quiet joy that is already within you.

This 12-week course is a practical, step-by-step journey into that experience. No prior knowledge is needed, and like every Sahaja Yoga course, it is completely free.

International, live and online

The course is held live at our meditation centre in Amsterdam and online at the same time. We have always attracted a very international audience, with English-speaking seekers joining not only from the Netherlands and the rest of Europe, but from around the world.
